Why negative log probability?

\max_{W,b}\prod_i p(y_i\mid x_i;W,b)

Taking a logarithm preserves the maximizer.

\max_{W,b}\sum_i\log p(y_i\mid x_i;W,b)
\Longleftrightarrow\quad\min_{W,b}\frac1N\sum_i-\log p(y_i\mid x_i;W,b)

The likelihood factorization treats examples as conditionally independent under the model.
