L1 and L2 define different neighborhoods

d_{\mathrm{L1}}(\mathbf q,\mathbf x^{(i)})=\sum_{j=1}^{D}|q_j-x_j^{(i)}|d_{\mathrm{L2}}(\mathbf q,\mathbf x^{(i)})=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^{D}(q_j-x_j^{(i)})^2}

Contours show d(\mathbf q,\mathbf 0)=1 in two dimensions.

L2 preserves distances under orthogonal rotations. L1 generally does not.
