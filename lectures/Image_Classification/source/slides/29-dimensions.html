The linear classifier

\underbrace{s}_{C\times1}=\underbrace{W}_{C\times D}\underbrace{x}_{D\times1}+\underbrace{b}_{C\times1}
s_c=w_c^\top x+b_c
InputCIFAR-10 image flattened to D=3072 values
ParametersW\in\mathbb R^{10\times3072},\quad b\in\mathbb R^{10}
OutputTen scores, one for each category

Strictly, the bias makes the mapping affine. “Linear classifier” is standard terminology.
