## Consequential adaptations

- The source lecture spans training and architecture. This 90-minute lecture prioritizes architecture; initialization, dropout, and transfer are concise supporting contracts. Inception and MobileNet extend the comparison rather than claiming to appear in the supplied architecture section.
- Slides use H×W×C without batch. PyTorch code explicitly uses N×C×H×W. Weight counts exclude biases and BN unless explicitly stated; MACs count only forward convolution per image. One MAC is approximately two FLOPs.
- “Receptive field” means theoretical support extent, distinguished from empirically measured influence. Equal extent does not imply functional equivalence.
- Residual diagrams use post-activation blocks, with the reused source illustrations identified above. The identity Jacobian is for the sum before ReLU; the activation mask and possible gradient cancellation are stated in notes. Projections contribute their own Jacobian.
- Dropout uses the inverted training-time scaling convention used by common frameworks; the supplied lecture illustrates test-time scaling. Both preserve the expected activation under their corresponding conventions.
- Global-pooling comparison is a controlled 1000-class head comparison, not the original VGG head. The bottleneck comparison also holds feature sizes fixed. No cross-model accuracy rankings or latency measurements are invented.

The original standalone objectives slide was removed at the instructor’s request. Stable IDs of all surviving slides are unchanged; visible pages and derived notes/maps were renumbered after deletions. Instructor-only planning outcomes remain aligned with assessment.
