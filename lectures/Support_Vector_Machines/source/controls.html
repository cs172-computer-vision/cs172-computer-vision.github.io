Presentation controls

Right / Space / Page DownNext reveal or slide
Left / Page UpPrevious reveal or slide
EndReveal the entire current slide
HomeFirst slide
TShow or hide the fixed control bar
O / N / F / BOverview / notes / full screen / black screen
EscClose a panel, black screen, or toolbar

The toolbar stays hidden during presentation. Move to the bottom edge to reveal it temporarily, or press T to show or hide it. It hides after 2.2 seconds away from its buttons. Its size stays fixed across slides.

For PDF: use Print / PDF, choose Save as PDF, enable background graphics, and disable browser headers and footers. All reveals print in their final state. For a guaranteed 16:9 version, use the supplied PDF.

Figures, fonts, equations, and interactions are bundled locally. Citation links require internet only when opened.
