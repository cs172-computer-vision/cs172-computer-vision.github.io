A brief look at other optimizers

MethodMain idea
MomentumAccumulate past gradients to smooth the direction.
AdaGrad / RMSPropScale coordinates using past squared gradients.
AdamCombine momentum and adaptive coordinate scaling.

They address issues such as narrow valleys, uneven scales, and gradient noise.

They still need a learning rate. No optimizer is best for every problem.
