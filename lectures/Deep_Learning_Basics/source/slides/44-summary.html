Five ideas to take away

  1. SVM loss: penalize competitors that violate a score margin.
  2. Regularization: add a preference over the weights.
  3. SGD: estimate the gradient with a minibatch, then step downhill.
  4. MLP: compose affine maps with nonlinear activations.
  5. Backpropagation: compute gradients by following the graph backward.
