Five ideas to take away

  1. Objective: combine classification loss and weight regularization.
  2. SGD: estimate a gradient from a minibatch, then update.
  3. MLP: compose learned affine maps and hidden features.
  4. Nonlinearity: activation functions prevent affine collapse.
  5. Backpropagation: compute gradients by following the graph backward.
