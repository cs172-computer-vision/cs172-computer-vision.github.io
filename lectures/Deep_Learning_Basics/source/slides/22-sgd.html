Stochastic gradient descent uses a minibatch

Sample a minibatch \mathcal B_t of B examples.

g_t=\frac1B\sum_{i\in\mathcal B_t}\nabla L_i(\theta_t)+\lambda\nabla\!\left(\tfrac12\sum_\ell\lVert W_\ell\rVert_F^2\right)\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\eta g_t

Uniform sampling gives an unbiased data-gradient estimate at fixed \theta_t.

“SGD” commonly includes minibatches; strict single-example SGD has B=1.
