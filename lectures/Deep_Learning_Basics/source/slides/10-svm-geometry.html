SVM also has a geometric interpretation

Binary labels t_i\in\{-1,+1\}.

t_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1

The two margin planes are separated by 2/\lVert w\rVert_2.

Smaller \lVert w\rVert_2 favors a wider geometric margin.

Support vectors lie on or inside the margin in the soft-margin formulation.
