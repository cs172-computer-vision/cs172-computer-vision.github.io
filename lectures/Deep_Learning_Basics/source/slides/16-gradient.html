The negative gradient points downhill

\nabla_\theta L=\left[\frac{\partial L}{\partial\theta_1},\ldots,\frac{\partial L}{\partial\theta_P}\right]^\topL(\theta+\delta)\approx L(\theta)+\nabla L(\theta)^\top\delta
\delta=-\eta\nabla L\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Delta L\approx-\eta\lVert\nabla L\rVert_2^2

For a sufficiently small positive step, move against the gradient.
