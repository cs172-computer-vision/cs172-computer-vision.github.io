Definitions
The blob has height 1 and width 2a. “Normalized” means scale normalization by σ²; the Gaussian has unit area in both modes.
Gσ(x)=12πσe−x22σ2 Gσ″(x)=x2−σ2σ4Gσ(x) r(x)=c[Gσ′(x+a)−Gσ′(x−a)]

c = 1 for unnormalized LoG; c = σ² for scale-normalized LoG. The plots use the conventional sign: a bright blob has a negative center response.
